# Article Refresh

Revitalize a blog post into its optimal version — what it could have been with perfect clarity, complete information available at the time, and ideal execution — while maintaining absolute temporal authenticity.

## Binding rules

1. **Absolute temporal integrity.** The post must read as written entirely on its original date: only knowledge, tools, terms and references that existed then, and predictions that would have seemed reasonable then.
2. **Preserve the author's voice, argument and quirks.** Added content must be indistinguishable from the original, and the whole post must feel unified.
3. **Start from the dated URL and the complete original**, with formatting and hyperlinks preserved; ask for anything missing.
4. **Keep every valuable hyperlink from the original** unless one is deliberately removed.
5. **Every image gets alt text**, written after viewing the image, never inferred from its filename.
6. **Verify that every fact, example and reference is period-appropriate** before delivery.
7. **No template.** Develop sections as unevenly as the content deserves, vary structure from post to post, and keep some natural imperfection.
8. **Deliver the complete enhanced post as a document artifact.**

## Quick Start

1. Obtain the blog post URL (contains the publication date) and its complete original content.
2. Apply the full framework in [references/framework.md](references/framework.md) to create an enhanced version that significantly improves value, clarity, and reader engagement while maintaining absolute temporal integrity.
3. The post must appear to have been written entirely on its original publication date.
4. Provide the complete enhanced post as a document artifact.

## Initial Information Required

Gather before starting (ask if not provided):

- The URL of the blog post (contains the publication date)
- The complete original content (pasted directly or uploaded with formatting preserved)
- Any supporting materials (PDF of full web page, markdown with hyperlinks preserved)
- Context about the original post's purpose, target audience, or performance metrics
- Any specific areas of concern or goals for the enhancement

## Implementation Process

### Phase 1: Deep Understanding

1. Read the original post multiple times, noting strengths and weaknesses
2. Research the temporal context and available knowledge at publication
3. Identify the core value proposition and target audience
4. Map voice characteristics and style patterns
5. Determine which enhancement strategies provide maximum value

### Phase 2: Strategic Enhancement

1. Develop a customized enhancement plan based on analysis
2. Apply improvements iteratively, maintaining temporal integrity
3. Ensure each addition strengthens rather than dilutes the message
4. Balance depth with readability and engagement
5. Integrate enhancements naturally with existing content

### Phase 3: Refinement and Verification

1. Review for temporal consistency and authenticity
2. Ensure voice and style remain consistent throughout
3. Verify all facts, examples, and references are period-appropriate
4. Check that the enhanced version fulfills the original's promise better
5. Polish for natural flow and compelling reader experience

## Final Quality Assurance

The revitalized post must:

- Deliver significantly more value while maintaining the original's essence
- Read as if the author had perfect clarity and unlimited time on the original date
- Maintain absolute temporal integrity with no anachronisms
- Preserve and amplify the author's unique voice and perspective
- Feel like natural, organic writing, not an artificial enhancement
- Include all valuable hyperlinks from the original (unless deliberately removed)
- Create a lasting resource that remains valuable beyond its publication date
- **Have alt text on every image.** A refresh is the natural moment to fix accessibility debt: if the article carries captionless images (`![](image.png)`), describe them now rather than leaving them for a separate cleanup. View the image before describing it — don't infer content from the filename.
