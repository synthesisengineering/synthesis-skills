# Content Distribution

Strategically promote content across social media platforms to build genuine thought leadership, spark meaningful discussion, and create valuable professional connections.

## Binding rules

1. **Brief before posts.** Present the strategic brief (content, audience, platforms, positioning) and get feedback before writing any post.
2. **Voice comes from the user's active voice profile, never from this skill.** If none is loaded, ask for one first; defaults produce posts that read as machine-generated.
3. **Write social posts in social register.** First person, conversational, no essay scaffolding or uppercase severity labels, no em dashes, and end by inviting a response.
4. **Hand over drafts for the human polish pass**, already carrying the four codifiable defaults: peer-stance closing question, learner-stance close on emerging topics, a comments hook on LinkedIn, restrained verbs.
5. **Never fake the human's part.** No inserted "haha" or emoji, no invented callbacks, no claimed emotions or expertise, no smoothing away real roughness.
6. **No promotional language**: no "honored/humbled/excited/thrilled", no asking for likes or shares, no engagement farming or thread announcements.
7. **Be open about authorship** on Hacker News and Reddit, and answer comments by adding value, never defensively.
8. **Save a published article's posts in a `social.md` sidecar beside it**, one section per platform, so they move with the article.

## Content Creation Process

### Step 1: Strategic Brief

Present analysis: what makes this content valuable, platform recommendations with rationale, suggested emphasis per platform, timing/sequencing, risks or sensitivities. Wait for feedback.

### Step 2: Content Creation

For each approved platform, provide:
- **Platform** name
- **Strategic Approach**: One paragraph explaining strategy
- **Post Content**: Actual text, formatted for the platform
- **Engagement Hook**: What will spark discussion
- **Success Indicators**: What good engagement looks like
- **Follow-up Strategy**: How to engage with responses

### Step 3: Cross-Platform Coordination

**Posting Sequence**: Which platform first and why. Spacing between platforms. Timing considerations.

**Cross-Pollination**: When to reference discussion from one platform on another. How to synthesize feedback. Opportunities for follow-up content.
