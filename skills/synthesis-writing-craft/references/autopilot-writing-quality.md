# Writing outcome review

Judge the actual reader's purpose, source fidelity, argument, structure and
requested voice. Preserve decisive detail and the source's causal mechanism.
Invented autobiography, unsupported attribution and persuasive prose that
changes a source's meaning are substantive defects.

Use reader briefing and framing before drafting; use article writing for an
article package, including its title, opening and reader promise. Apply factual
checking, content quality, writing pitfalls and craft together. Load an author's
voice profile only when authorized for the task. Public defaults do not import
another user's private style or personal history.

Consider meaningfully different approaches when the brief leaves the creative
choice open. Compare the alternatives against the reader's need and choose one;
do not create a required number of variants for a decided correction. Retain
specificity, imagery, rhythm and deliberate fragments that serve the piece.
The strongest version can be less uniform than the editor's preferred style.

A rejection needs an explicit requirement violation or concrete reader harm,
with exact source/output evidence. Label a stylistic preference separately.
Assess distinctive insight against the brief and reader purpose; familiar or
unfamiliar wording alone does not establish its quality.
Unconventional syntax, dialect, accessible plain language and unfamiliar voice
are not defects by themselves. Never infer authorship or model provenance from
style cues. Use a sound control with the intended distinctive voice and seeded
defects in factual fidelity, purpose, structure and actual voice requirements.
Measure false rejection as well as missed defects. A reviewer who polishes away
the good control has failed calibration.

Repair the specific defect and reassess the affected passage in its full reader
context. Preserve the prior verdict and original controls. Repeatedly replacing
reviewers until one likes the prose does not establish improvement. A calibrated
review on these controls is limited to this task; it is not a claim of general
editorial accuracy or superiority over a native client.
