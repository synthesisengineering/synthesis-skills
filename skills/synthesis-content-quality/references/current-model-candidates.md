## Controlled-test quarantine

The following candidates are research inputs, not active detector rules or
family fingerprints. Do not attach prevalence, family attribution, or a
production threshold until the stated comparison is complete.

| Candidate | Evidence now | Required test before promotion |
|---|---|---|
| Compact surface plus abstract lexical load | One exact GPT-5.6 Sol practitioner benchmark plus a documented concise family baseline | Matched prompts across Sol, Terra, Luna, current Claude, Rajiv's archive, and professional controls; joint readability and abstraction measures. |
| Source mechanism blurred into a familiar thesis | One exact Sol editorial benchmark | Annotated source packets; preservation of names, quantities, causal steps, constraints, and intended claim. |
| Edit-scope inflation | Peer-reviewed older-model proofreading result plus exact Sol practitioner observation | Gold scopes for proofreading, grammar, clarity, line, developmental, and voice-preserving edits; proposition and voice preservation. |
| Dressed-up copulas without semantic gain | Practitioner and vendor evidence plus older corpus research | Blinded comparison of matched edit pairs; count only substitutions where the plain copula is more accurate and clear. |
| Notability and media-coverage recitation | Current field evidence and vendor examples | Matched biography/company prompts; source entailment and outlet-name density by genre. |
| Source-style amplification | Cross-model peer-reviewed dialogue research | Seeded source quirks under preserve-voice and normalize-to-house-style conditions. |
| Corpus-level expressive and information-flow sameness | Peer-reviewed cross-model research | Same-genre comparison against Rajiv's established human archive, controlling for house style and sample size. |

## Rejected as prose fingerprints

- Sonnet 5 literal-scope inconsistency is a task-specification and acceptance
  issue, not a stable textual fingerprint.
- Context-budget self-handoff language belongs in context lifecycle and
  execution controls.
- Small tables, awards headings, and platform-specific heading quirks are
  venue conventions, not portable authorship evidence.
- No word, dash, triplet, compact paragraph, or dressed-up verb identifies a
  provider by itself.

## Promotion gate

Before any quarantined candidate becomes an active rule:

1. Predefine the hypothesis, units, metrics, and promotion threshold.
2. Record exact model, surface, date, effort, prompt, genre, and source packet.
3. Include same-task human controls and counterexamples.
4. Report raw counts and denominators; use uncertainty intervals for rate
   comparisons.
5. Separate editorial harm, style cue, technical provenance, and authorship.
6. Add rather than replace; any removal or demotion requires Rajiv's explicit
   approval.
