# Critical deadlines at intake

Read the day a deadline whose miss cannot be undone is first seen, and whenever one closes. The
plan grammar, the sweep, the alerts and the ritual record are the daily-rituals skill's
(`synthesis-daily-rituals`, references/critical-deadlines.md); this file is the chief of staff's
part: recognizing one, getting it onto the calendar, and closing it.

## What counts

The preferences name the principal's classes (`critical_deadlines.classes`). The usual ones:
benefits and insurance enrollment, taxes, legal and patent filings, immigration and identity
documents, pay, time-off and award actions for the principal's reports, and account-security
checks. The test is the harm, not the sender or the tone: if missing the date cannot be undone,
or cannot be fixed until the next cycle, it is critical, however routine the announcement reads.

## The same day it is first seen

1. **Tag it** in the day plan: `**Critical:** <class>`, `**Decays:** <closing date>`, a
   `**Decay ID:**`, and the direct link with the exact closing time in the principal's zone.
2. **Put it on the calendar:** an all-day marker on the closing date, and a working block early
   in the window at a time that passes the slot check
   ([scheduling-protocol.md](scheduling-protocol.md)). Both are entries with no attendees, so
   they need no approval. Write them on the item's `**Calendar:**` line.
3. **Say so in that day's brief,** first. From seven days before the date it leads every plan,
   brief and summary until it closes.

Never fold it into a decision packet, a batch of small asks or a list of dated tasks. Never
write it up as optional, and never decide for the principal that nothing is needed: an
enrollment may need an active election or a re-verification even when nothing else changes.

## Closing it

It closes on evidence: the confirmation (with its time), or the principal's own words quoted
with the time they said them. Record that on the item's `**Evidence:**` line with the closing
mark. If the principal says they are letting it go, that is their decision, recorded in their
words. When an annual deadline closes, put next year's reminder on the calendar the same day,
early enough to open the window rather than close it.

## Config

`critical_deadlines` in the preferences: `classes` (what counts for this principal), `calendar`
(which calendar takes the marker and the working block), and any standing reminders already
planted. The example file ships the block with generic values.
