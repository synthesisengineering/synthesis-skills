# Writing pitfalls: background

Why this skill stands apart from AI-pattern detection, the philosophy under every fix, and the sibling skills.

## Why This Is a Separate Skill

Bad writing has two distinct root-cause classes that need different detection and different fixes:

- **AI-generation patterns** (covered by `synthesis-content-quality`): statistical regression to the mean, training-data bias, optimization for blandness. Fix is to inject specificity, voice, and expertise.
- **Human-source patterns** (covered HERE): social and psychological insecurity, defensive posturing, performance of credibility, laziness, lack of confident voice. Fix is to remove defensiveness, trust the reader, and state the substance plainly.

These overlap in surface symptoms (a humble-brag from a human reads similar to a sycophantic phrase from an AI), but the root cause and the fix differ. Treating them as one category obscures both.

## Core Philosophy

The patterns in this catalog are rooted in writer-reader dynamics that have existed for centuries. They predate AI and exist independently of it. The fix in every case is some version of: trust the reader, drop the defense, state the substance plainly.

A pattern is identified by its function (what is the writer doing that weakens the prose?), not by surface form. The same words can be a pitfall in one context and appropriate in another. Calibrate by venue, audience, and intent.

## Distinct from AI-Pattern Detection

This skill catches HUMAN-SOURCE failures. For AI-generation patterns (saturated vocabulary clustering, exhausted metaphors as structural filler, the concierge tone, hyperbolic subheadings, the rule of three, mechanical transitions, etc.), use [`synthesis-content-quality`](../../synthesis-content-quality/SKILL.md). The two skills together cover the bad-writing landscape from both root-cause directions.

Surface prose alone does not establish authorship. Classify the observable pattern, explain its effect, and revise the prose; do not convert a stylistic cue into a claim that a human or a model wrote it.

For positive principles (what to DO instead of what to avoid), use [`synthesis-writing-craft`](../../synthesis-writing-craft/SKILL.md).
